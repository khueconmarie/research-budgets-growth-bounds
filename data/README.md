# Accounting observations

The public data are transcribed from the two official HKEX prospectuses listed in `sources.json`. Page numbers in that file count PDF pages from one. The main expense tables are MiniMax PDF page 433 (printed page 423) and Knowledge Atlas/Zhipu PDF pages 266-267 (printed pages 257-258).

Each row records a company, fiscal or interim period, currency, raw computing expense, R&D total, and component breakdown. The inference-cost categories in cost of sales are outside the research-computing numerator. Every accepted component sum matches its reported R&D total.

MiniMax's printed computing percentages for 2022 and 2023 are 39.4% and 67.6%. Dividing the raw amounts gives 39.2898% and 67.4681%. Both printed entries remain in the JSON; calculations use ratios of raw amounts. Differences are not silently corrected in the transcription.

All amounts are in thousands of the row's currency. Ratios are within-row. No currency conversion, temporal pooling, or annualization is performed. The 2024 interim columns carry the source's unaudited designation. The 2025 annual reports serve as cross-checks of 2024 R&D totals, not as a source for an unreported full-year 2025 computing breakdown.

The minimum observed compute share is `14633 / 84377`. Transferring that historical observed-set floor to future modeled research requires the relative-valuation, coverage, aggregation, and persistence restrictions described in the manuscript. The observations alone do not estimate those restrictions.
